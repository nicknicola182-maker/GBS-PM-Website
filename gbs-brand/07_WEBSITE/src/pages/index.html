Specialism

Fire door inspection, remedial works and installation

Fire doors only perform when every component is right: leaf, frame, gaps, intumescent strips and smoke seals, closer, hinges, ironmongery, glazing and signage. We assess each assembly against measurable tolerances and recommend the most proportionate route to compliance.

  • Inspection of fire door assemblies against BS 8214, Approved Document B and manufacturer instructions.
  • Defect-by-defect reports with photographs, measurements and a priced remedial schedule.
  • Compliant repair before replacement wherever the door can be brought back to standard.
  • FD30 and FD60 door set supply and installation where replacement is the right answer.
  • Maintenance programmes and records that support quarterly communal door checks and annual flat entrance door checks under the Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022.
