Cookies and website analytics

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising scripts. Fonts and all other files are served from our own domain. Our hosting provider records standard server logs, including IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and performance; these are retained for a limited period and are not used to identify visitors.

If you submit the quote request form, it is passed to us by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and our email delivery provider (Resend), who act as our processors and use it only to deliver the enquiry to our inbox. If that service is unavailable the form falls back to your own email client.

Who we share data with

We share personal data only where needed to deliver our services: with subcontractors and suppliers working on a job, with managing agents, landlords and insurers who have instructed the works, with our accountants, insurers and professional advisers, and with IT and hosting providers who process data on our instructions. We do not sell personal data. Where a provider is outside the UK, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement.

How long we keep data

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Job records, reports, photographs and invoices are kept for 6 years after the end of the financial year in which the work was completed, to meet contractual, tax and limitation requirements. Fire door inspection records may be kept longer where they form part of a building's fire safety file.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to its use, or receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time. To exercise any right, email {{company.email}}. We will respond within one month.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to resolve any concern first.

Security

Data is held on access-controlled systems. Site photographs are taken only of the works and building fabric; we avoid capturing people, personal belongings or documents wherever practicable.
