What we do

Every property needs practical care, whether it is your own home, a shared residential building or a portfolio you manage. We deliver planned maintenance, reactive repairs and larger improvement works, bringing a broad range of trades together.

Our work covers general building maintenance, electrical and plumbing, kitchens and bathrooms, joinery, decorating, roofing, windows, drainage, groundworks, fire doors, firestopping and more.

We work closely with managing agents and property managers, and are an approved contractor for a number of property management companies. We equally welcome individuals who need work in their own house or flat. The same clear approach applies: understand the requirement, agree the scope and communicate throughout.
