How we work

Clear at every stage,
from first enquiry to final report.

One clear process, whether the work is in your own home or across the buildings you manage.

01

Enquiry and survey

Tell us about the property, the issue and any access arrangements. We survey and confirm what is needed.

02

Scope and cost plan

A clear scope and quotation for the works, with options where appropriate.

03

Works and reporting

Work to the agreed scope, with risk assessments and method statements as needed, followed by a report and photographs.

04

Follow-on and maintenance

Agree follow-on work and variations before they are carried out, with planned care where it adds value.
