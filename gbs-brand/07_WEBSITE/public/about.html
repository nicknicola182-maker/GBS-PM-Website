About GBS

Built around your property.
And the people behind it.

GBS Property Maintenance provides maintenance, repairs and project works for homeowners, landlords, managing agents and commercial clients across London and the surrounding Home Counties.

What we do

Every property needs practical care, whether it is your own home, a shared residential building or a portfolio you manage. We deliver planned maintenance, reactive repairs and larger improvement works, bringing a broad range of trades together.

Our work covers general building maintenance, electrical and plumbing, kitchens and bathrooms, joinery, decorating, roofing, windows, drainage, groundworks, fire doors, firestopping and more.

We work closely with managing agents and property managers, and are an approved contractor for a number of property management companies. We equally welcome individuals who need work in their own house or flat. The same clear approach applies: understand the requirement, agree the scope and communicate throughout.

How we work

  • A clear scope and quotation for the works before anything starts.
  • Any allowances explained up front, with options where there is a choice.

  • Variations discussed before additional work is carried out.
  • Reports and photographs on completion, with respectful working in occupied homes and buildings.

The company

Directly managed, accountable and easy to deal with

GBS Property Maintenance is an independent contractor run by its own management team. Every scope, quotation and programme is reviewed by management before it is issued, and clients deal with decision-makers rather than a call centre.

Enquiries are handled through the office: 020 8051 9333 and info@gbs-pm.co.uk.

Health, safety and insurance

Safe systems of work as standard

Risk assessments and method statements are issued before every job. Operatives work to site rules, permit systems and the building's fire strategy, and statutory certification is provided by certified partners where the works require it.

Public liability and employer's liability insurance held. Certificates available on request.
