# 06_SIGNAGE - site, hoarding, entrance and notice artwork

Signage artwork for GBS Property Maintenance, generated by `tools/render.mjs tools/manifests/06_signage.json`. Each item has a `_SCREEN.pdf` at trim size and a `_PRESS.pdf` with bleed and crop marks. Contact details and the legal line are substituted from `company.json` at render time; the bracketed fields ([Client / managing agent], [Scope of works], [Building name], [Dates], [Description of works], [Access arrangements]) are completed per job by the printer or in the office before printing.

## Substrates (production manual, signage section)

- Site boards 600 x 400 and 1200 x 800: 3 mm aluminium composite (ACM) with the artwork direct printed in UV-resistant inks and a UV laminate, or cut polymeric vinyl on painted aluminium for the simple elements. 5 mm foam PVC is acceptable only for boards that come down within a few weeks. Corners radiused 10 mm; no exposed raw ACM edge facing the public side.
- Hoarding panel 2440 x 1220: 3 mm ACM direct printed, or polymeric vinyl (with UV and anti-graffiti laminate) applied to the painted hoarding sheet or to ACM. Off-white ground and sage band must be printed, not painted on site, so the colours match the swatches.
- Estate entrance / office sign 400 x 300: 3 mm painted aluminium or ACM in the brand sage (printed, with laminate), reversed logo in cut off-white and red polymeric vinyl or direct printed. Option: 3 mm acrylic with stand-off fixings for office interiors.
- A4 works notice: office print on 100-120 gsm, or sealed in a laminating pouch when it goes outdoors or in a lift lobby.
- All print: UV-resistant pigment inks, colour matched to the approved physical swatches (sage #6E7F72, red #E10600, off-white #F9F9F7; digital values are provisional). Red stays an accent: pipes, bars and rules only.

## Fixing

- Boards on temporary fencing: at least four fixings through eyelets or drilled holes 15 mm in from the corners, with UV-stable cable ties or bolted sign clips; mount at 1.2-1.8 m to the centre; face away from the highway if the fence is at the kerb.
- Boards on walls or hoardings: stand-off or screw fixings through cup washers at 300 mm centres along the edges; never fix through the printed face of a cut-vinyl element. Confirm the wall owner's consent and make good on removal.
- Hoarding panel: screw to the hoarding frame at 300 mm centres, panel joints sealed; keep the bottom edge at least 150 mm above ground to avoid splash damage; check wind loading on long runs.
- Entrance / office sign: four stand-off fixings or concealed split battens; outdoor fixings in stainless steel.
- All signs must stay clear of sight lines, fire exits, fire-fighting equipment, meters and existing statutory signage.

## Consent and compliance

- Obtain written consent from the client or managing agent before any sign goes up on their land or building, and agree the removal date.
- Temporary contractor's boards on the land where the works are carried out generally fall within deemed consent under the Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) (England) Regulations 2007, Schedule 3 Class 3, subject to limits on size, number and duration. Check the current limits, and any conservation-area or listed-building restrictions, with the local planning authority before display, and remove the sign within 14 days of completion of the works.
- A hoarding on or over the public highway needs a hoarding licence from the highway authority; the artwork on it does not replace the statutory site safety signage.
- The "Contractor on site" strip is displayed only while GBS personnel are working on the site.
- The legal line (legal name, place of registration, company number, registered office, VAT number) is already on the site boards and the works notice and must not be removed.
