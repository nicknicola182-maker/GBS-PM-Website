# 05_VEHICLES - van livery

Livery layout and full-scale vector panels for the GBS Property Maintenance white vans (medium-wheelbase high-roof panel van, Transit Custom / Vivaro class). Generated by `tools/render.mjs tools/manifests/05_vehicles.json` from the logo masters and `company.json`.

Every vehicle dimension in this folder is a placeholder taken from a generic 5300 x 2000 mm elevation. Nothing is cut until the actual van has been surveyed and the layout re-checked against real panel sizes.

## Scale notes

- Sheets 1 and 2 (side elevations) are drawn at 1:15 on A3, because a 5.3 m van does not fit an A3 sheet at 1:10. Sheets 3 and 4 (rear and front) are at 1:10. Every sheet carries a 1000 mm scale bar.
- Dimensions on the sheets are full-scale millimetres regardless of the drawing scale. Scale from the dimensions, not from the paper.
- The SVG panels are true size: `width` and `height` are in mm and one user unit in the `viewBox` is one millimetre. Import at 100 % and check one dimension against the table above before nesting.
- Fonts: the contact panels are already outlined from Inter SemiBold (no live text). If the text is ever re-set (new number, new address), set it in Inter SemiBold from `00_BRAND_CORE/fonts/Inter-600.ttf`, outline it to paths before cutting and never send a cut file with live fonts. The logo panels are outlined vectors as supplied.

## Supplier checklist

1. Survey the vehicle: measure every item in the table above, photograph each side with a tape in shot, map swage lines, door gaps, handles, track rails, lamps, sensors, cameras and badges. Confirm the fuel-flap side and the number of sliding doors.
2. Re-set the layout to the survey and send a dimensioned proof (PDF) for approval before any cutting.
3. Materials: cast polymeric vinyl with matching cast laminate on every printed element; cast coloured film for cut lettering and bars. Sage and red to the approved physical swatches; send film draw-downs for sign-off. One finish (gloss or satin) across the vehicle. No metallic, reflective or chrome films.
4. Cut files: layers named LOGO, CONTACT, BARS, CUT-DOOR-GAP, KEEP-CLEAR and REGMARKS. Minimum cut stroke 3 mm. All text outlined. Report nest sizes and number of pieces with the quote.
5. Keep clear of lamps, number plates, sensors, cameras, wipers, washer jets, badges, handles, locks, rubbing strips and all door gaps and track rails. Nothing on bumpers or glass. 50 mm minimum to any panel edge.
6. First vehicle: full-size paper or low-tack proof of the side lockup and the rear doors on the van, photographed from 5 m and 15 m, signed off by the GBS Director before vinyl is cut (production register item V01).
7. Application dry, on clean degreased panels, within the film maker's temperature range; post-heat edges and recesses. Mirror the position (not the artwork) between nearside and offside.
8. Hand over: photo set of all four faces plus close-ups, film batch numbers, aftercare note (no pressure washing closer than 300 mm; no jet washing of edges for 7 days).

## Regenerate

```
python3 -I 05_VEHICLES/build-livery-panels.py        # SVG panels from the logo masters and company.json (needs fontTools)
python3 -I 05_VEHICLES/build-livery-layout.py        # rewrites livery-layout.html
node tools/render.mjs tools/manifests/05_vehicles.json
node tools/qa-overflow.mjs 05_VEHICLES/livery-layout.html 8
```

Run the panel generator whenever the logo masters or the contact details in `company.json` change; the contact panels are outlined with Inter SemiBold at generation time, so no font is needed downstream.
