  • Confirm the fuel-flap side and the number of sliding doors on the actual vehicle.

Fabrication notes and schedule

Read with the production manual, section 06
V01
Sheet 5 of 5
v1.0

Materials

  • Cast polymeric vinyl (not calendered) for every element, with a matching cast laminate over all printed vinyl. Cut-vinyl lettering and bars in cast coloured film; no laminate needed on single-colour cut film unless the installer specifies it.
  • Colours: sage and red to the approved physical swatches (manual section 02). Supply draw-downs of the proposed films for sign-off before cutting; the digital values are provisional.
  • Finish: gloss or satin to match across all elements on one vehicle. No chrome, metallic, reflective or textured films.

Artwork

  • All panels are supplied as SVG at full vehicle scale, one millimetre per user unit, with the logo as outlined paths. Text panels (contact row and contact block) are already outlined from Inter SemiBold; if any text is re-set, outline it to paths before cutting and keep the font files (00_BRAND_CORE/fonts) out of the cut file.
  • Keep the logo proportions, colour split and clear space exactly as supplied. Do not add keylines, shadows, gradients, rules or a white backing shape on a white vehicle.
  • Place cut contours on named layers: LOGO, CONTACT, BARS, CUT-DOOR-GAP, KEEP-CLEAR. Regmarks on a separate layer. Report nest sizes and the number of pieces with the quote.
  • Minimum cut-vinyl stroke 3 mm; the descriptor and strapline cut as one piece per word, with application tape.

Vehicle and survey

  • Vehicle: Citroën Dispatch XL panel van, 2026 model year (third generation, 2024 facelift), white, standard roof. Elevations are drawn to Citroën's published dimensions (5331 x 1920 x 1935 mm, wheelbase 3275 mm); panel shapes, crease and rail positions are approximate. Replace the outline with the manufacturer template (GetOutlines, Citroën Jumpy / Dispatch 3rd generation facelift panel van XL) when purchased, and cut nothing until the survey sheet has real measurements, photographs with a tape in shot and the panel map.
  • Record: panel widths between gaps, swage lines, roof line, sill, wheel-arch tops, sliding-door track rails and travel, fuel-flap side, lamp wrap-around, sensors, cameras, plate position, glazing.
  • Keep clear of lamps, number plates, sensors, cameras, wipers, washer jets, badges, handles, locks, rubbing strips and all door gaps. Nothing on bumpers or glass.

Proof and installation

  • First vehicle: full-size paper or low-tack proof of the side lockup and the rear doors on the vehicle, photographed from 5 m and 15 m and signed off by the GBS Director before vinyl is cut.
  • Apply dry on a clean, degreased, dry panel at the film manufacturer's temperature range. Post-heat edges and any recess as specified. Replicate the nearside layout on the offside as a mirror of position, never of the artwork.
  • Hand over a photo set (both sides, rear, front, close-ups), the film batch numbers and the aftercare note (no pressure washing closer than 300 mm, no jet washing of edges for 7 days).
