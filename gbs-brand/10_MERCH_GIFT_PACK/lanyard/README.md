| Print method | Dye-sublimation on 20 mm polyester, printed both sides, with the same artwork on the reverse. Woven lanyards cannot reproduce the thin pipes; if a woven version is wanted, drop the strapline and use "GBS · PROPERTY MAINTENANCE" only. |
| Fittings | Safety breakaway at the neck, metal trigger clip or swivel hook, optional plastic buckle. Fittings are chosen on the supplier's order form; they are not part of the artwork. |

Notes

- The red is used on sage here in the same way as the approved reversed logo (red descriptor and pipes on a sage ground). Keep it to the pipes only.
- Ask for a printed strike-off before the run. Dye-sub sage can shift warm; compare with the mug or notebook cover.
- Text sits 8.6 mm from the top edge, so it survives the supplier's usual 2-3 mm stitching margin.
