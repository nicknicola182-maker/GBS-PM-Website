| Colours | Sage #6E7F72, red #E10600 on white. Two spot colours if screen printed. |

Notes

- Use only decals and adhesives the hard hat manufacturer approves for their shell. Solvent-based adhesives can weaken polyethylene shells. The supplier should confirm the adhesive is suitable for PPE.
- Place on the front centre or the side above the brim. Never cover the shell's CE / UKCA marking, the manufacturing date or the inspection label.
- The 2 mm bleed is set in the HTML (`--bleed: 2mm`) and in the manifest; `--marks` is raised to 7 mm so the renderer's crop marks still fall on the trim line. Keep those two values together if the size changes.
