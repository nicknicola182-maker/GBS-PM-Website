Notes

- Side B is at the lower limit for the strip lockup (descriptor about 1 mm high). Approve a sample before the run; if it does not hold, replace side B with the wordmark at 30 mm wide.
- The hole or ring position is set by the supplier's blank. Keep it at least 4 mm from the trim and away from the icon.
