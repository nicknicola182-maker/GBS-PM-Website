Notes

- The wrap is a flat 200 x 90 mm strip. The two ends meet at the handle. A centred logo sits opposite the handle and is visible from both sides when held. If the supplier offers a right-handed or left-handed layout, ask them to shift the logo so it faces outward when the mug is held in the right hand (logo centre at about 50 mm from the left end) and send a revised file; do not let them scale the logo.
- Keep the printable area clear of the handle join: most suppliers reserve 10 mm at each end. Nothing in this artwork sits within 20 mm of either end except the small web address, which is 10 mm in; move it if the supplier's template requires.
- Minimum order and dishwasher-safe coating to be confirmed in the supplier's quotation.
