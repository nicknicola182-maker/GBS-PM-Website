White T-shirt

W01-A
Sheet 1 of 7
v1.0

Garment

White or off-white garment

Technique

Screen print (2 spot colours, sage + red) for runs of 25 or more; DTF transfer for small runs and re-orders. Soft-hand ink, no underbase on white.

Placement

Chest mark: wearer's left, centre 105 mm from centre front, top edge 90 mm below the shoulder seam at the neck point, 95 mm wide. Sleeve emblem: wearer's left sleeve, centred on the outer sleeve, top edge 100 mm below the shoulder seam, 40 mm high. Back: centred on centre back, top edge 110 mm below the back neck seam, 260 mm wide.

Hi-vis vest (yellow, EN ISO 20471)

W03-B
Sheet 6 of 7
v1.0

Garment

Fluorescent yellow garment, mono black artwork

Technique

Screen print or transfer in black. Position to be agreed with the manufacturer so that no print touches the retroreflective tape and the certified fluorescent area is kept. Supplier to confirm the garment stays within its EN ISO 20471 class after printing.

Placement

Chest mark: wearer's left, centred between the front opening and the shoulder band (60 mm from centre front on this drawing), top edge 170 mm below the shoulder seam at the neck point, 95 mm wide. Back: centred on centre back, centred in the clear zone between the two horizontal bands (150 mm deep on this drawing), 260 mm wide. Final positions to be agreed with the manufacturer.

Embroidery brief

W02
Sheet 7 of 7
v1.0

Scope

Polo shirts (white and sage): left chest lockup and left sleeve emblem; optional back lockup only when ordered. Softshell jackets are heat transfer by default; embroider only if agreed in writing, using the same files.

Artwork supplied (vector, outlined, no live fonts)

  • 01_LOGO_MASTER/svg/GBS_HORIZON_LOGO_PRIMARY-NOSTRAP_SAGE-RED_v1.0.svg (white polo chest, 95 mm wide; optional back 230 mm)
  • 01_LOGO_MASTER/svg/GBS_HORIZON_LOGO_REVERSED-NOSTRAP_OFFWHITE-RED_v1.0.svg (sage polo chest, 95 mm wide; optional back 230 mm)
  • 01_LOGO_MASTER/svg/GBS_HORIZON_ICON_SIMPLIFIED_SAGE-RED_v1.0.svg and _OFFWHITE-RED (sleeve emblem, 40 mm high)
  • 04_WORKWEAR/GBS_HORIZON_WORKWEAR_CHEST_95MM_*.png and SLEEVE_40MM_*.png (300 dpi size references only; digitise from the SVG)

Digitise from the vector files without redrawing. Keep the proportions, the clear space (one GBS cap height on every side) and the colour split exactly as supplied. In the lockup, sew the second (hollow) bar of the icon as a solid bar, matching the simplified icon: its 1 mm outline will not sew at chest size.

Propose the two nearest 40 wt polyester shades for each colour and submit thread cards. Final shades are approved against the physical samples held by GBS, not against screen values. No metallic or high-sheen threads.

Minimum letter height test

Before production, sew out the descriptor PROPERTY MAINTENANCE at 5 mm and 6 mm cap height and at the actual chest size (3.9 mm at 95 mm wide; 4.3 mm at 105 mm) on the production fabric with the proposed stabiliser. If the 3.9 mm descriptor does not resolve cleanly, GBS will approve one of: (a) chest mark at 105 mm wide; (b) the wordmark without descriptor (01_LOGO_MASTER/svg/GBS_HORIZON_LOGO_WORDMARK_*) at 95 mm for embroidered chest marks. The descriptor must not be re-set in a larger size or a different font.

Sign-off

Supply a sew-out of every mark on the actual garment colour for approval by the GBS Director before the production run (asset W02 in the production register). Keep the stitch files and report stitch counts with the quote.
